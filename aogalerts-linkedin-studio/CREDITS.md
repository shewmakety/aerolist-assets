# Credits

## Logo

Full-color logo downloaded from https://assets.aogalerts.com/brand/aog-alerts-logo-20261113-1200x539.png and saved as `logos/aog-alerts-logo.png`.

`logos/aog-alerts-logo-white.png` is a white reverse made by recoloring the navy shapes to white and keeping the red. `logos/aog-alerts-tail.png` and `logos/aog-alerts-tail-white.png` are crops of the tail swoosh without the wordmark. Regenerate them with `python3 scripts/make-logo-variants.py`.

## Font

Inter Medium (weight 500) and Inter ExtraBold (weight 800), official woff2 files from the Inter 4.1 release (https://github.com/rsms/inter). Licensed under the SIL Open Font License. See `fonts/OFL.txt`.
